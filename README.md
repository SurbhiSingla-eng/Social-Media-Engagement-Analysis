Social media has become a major source of user engagement data, such as likes, shares, comments, and page views in today's digital age. These are important content efficiency metrics and reflect deeper user behaviour understandings in different digital environments. This project systematically statistically looks at 5,000 social media posts to uncover patterns and confirm statistical significance. This study presents a thorough data science pipeline that spans from data preprocessing to fine-grained time-series forecasting, delivering a sound solution framework applicable to data-driven decision-making and optimizing digital marketing and engagement strategies.
